About

I came to sustainability from two directions. I studied Philosophy (social and political) at UGM, where I got used to looking at institutions, interests, and the reasoning behind decisions.

Then I worked in procurement contracts at a nickel downstream industrial park. That job showed me how much of a business runs on contracts, payments, data, and many teams agreeing on the same thing.

My MBA brought the two together around one question: how can a carbon-intensive company keep growing while it learns to decarbonise? That's the kind of question I want to keep working on, in a company, a research team, or on project-based work. Happy to connect.
